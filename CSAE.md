# Child Safety Standards Policy

Publication of the Child Safety Standards

As a responsible company committed to creating a safe and positive community for all users, especially young people, the developer welcomes the introduction of the Child Safety Standards Policy on the Google Play Store. This policy is an important step in ensuring that apps offered on the platform meet the highest standards for protecting children and young people.

## Objectives of the Child Safety Standards Policy

The Child Safety Standards Policy pursues several key objectives that we as a company support and actively implement:

## Reporting System

Users can report harmful or development-impairing content in the app with just a few clicks.

## Protection of Privacy

We respect the privacy of our users and ensure that no personal data is collected from users.
Privacy Policy link: [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md)

## Age Verification

Registration on the app is only available for users aged 18 and above.

## Moderation System

The robust moderation system powered by third-party AI, ensures that harmful or development-impairing content is removed promptly and does not remain publicly visible for long.

## Images and Videos

Third party AI-powered algorithm reviews all images and videos before publication and blocks inappropriate content when necessary.

We are committed to removing content unsuitable for children and young people from our platform.

## Grooming

We enforce a zero-tolerance policy towards all forms of cyber grooming. If any suspicion arises, we take immediate and comprehensive action. This includes restricting the communication capabilities of the suspected account and conducting a thorough review of the reported behavior by our safety team. If the suspicion is confirmed, the account will be permanently banned, and the case may be referred to the relevant authorities.
We are committed to removing content unsuitable for children and young people from our platform.

## Advertising and Monetization

There is no monetization and advertising in the app.

## Community Safety

The safety of children and young people is our top priority. By adhering to the Child Safety Standards Policy, we are actively helping to create a safe and positive experience for all users in our community. We encourage our users to participate in fostering a respectful and secure environment.

For more information on the Child Safety Standards Policy and specific requirements, please visit the official Google Play Developer Policy page.
